# ColdStart — Site Positioning: The Flight Plan

The site is a journey. The visitor climbs through a futuristic stratosphere; content modules fly toward them as they scroll. Every chapter is one altitude band, one idea, one emotional beat. Copy is short because the modules are read mid-flight.

**Voice rules apply everywhere** (from the ColdStart brand voice guide): contractions always, arrow bullets, specific numbers, no banned words (leverage/streamline/optimize/etc.), sounds like John talking, not a brochure.

**Hard limits per module:** heading ≤6 words · body ≤40 words · ≤3 arrow bullets. These are engineering constraints of the dwell window, not style preferences.
